App apporteurs d'affaires · iOS

This is the SwiftUI app from branch claude/energy-courtage-app-plan-oj5xmb, redrawn for the browser with its own colours, copy and demo data. It's the same state the simulator opens on with -demo.

À essayer
  • Reco: tap the speech bubble next to a stage to read the broker's comment.
  • Switch to Entreprise, open the second card and press Valider l'étape until it reaches Devis signé. The 1000 € reward appears.
  • Accueil: Nouvelle recommandation. Type 06 75 75 75 75 as the phone number to trigger the duplicate check.
  • Voir le contrat: sign the invoice as the second party.
  • Profil: read the mandat de facturation to the end, sign it, and the warning on Accueil goes away.
  • Chat: open a conversation and send a message.

This is a browser preview, not the compiled app. The container this was built in has no Swift toolchain, so the iOS build has only been checked by CI on macOS. Nothing here talks to Supabase.
