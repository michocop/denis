## What to ask the lawyer

1. Is the **apport d'affaires** relationship correctly characterised, and does
   it create any risk of requalification as employment?
2. Does the self-billing mandate meet the conditions of art. 289 CGI?
3. Is the **consent basis for the filleul's data** the right one, or should it
   be legitimate interest with an information notice?
4. Does the activity fall under any **courtage en énergie** specific obligation
   (mandate, remuneration transparency)?
5. Retention: how long may a lost lead's personal data be kept?
