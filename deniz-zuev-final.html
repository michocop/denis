FORGED IN COMBAT

Born April 3, 1988, Dzianis "Deniz" Zuev is a Belarusian welterweight kickboxer whose professional career began in 2012. His journey through the world's most prestigious fighting circuits has shaped him into one of the most technically refined coaches in the Middle East.

"Victory is not given. It is earned through discipline, sacrifice, and the relentless pursuit of excellence."

Now based in Dubai, Deniz brings his championship mindset and elite-level training methodologies to fighters and fitness enthusiasts who demand the very best. Consistently ranked in Combat Press's Top 10 lightweights (2017, 2021), his technical mastery now fuels every session.
